This is the first article on my new site. From here on, this is where I'll publish longer pieces I want to keep in one place, instead of leaving them scattered across notes and documents.

A few things about how the site works:

- **Every article is on the front page**, newest first. Use the search bar (or press `/`) to find one by title or topic.
- **Topics** groups articles by tag, and the **Archive** lists them by year.
- **To get new articles as they're published**, subscribe to the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).

Thanks for reading.
